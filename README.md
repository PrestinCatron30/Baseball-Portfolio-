# Baseball-Portfolio-

Braves and Rangers trade proposal

Rangers pitcher Kumar Rocker to Atlanta for Ethan Bagwell, Luis Guanipa and Isaiah Drake

The Braves can afford to lose both of Guanipa and Drake due to the ascendance of 
Eric Hartman, Conor Essenburg and Owen Carey.

Once Atlanta acquires Kumar in my opinion they should focus on him using his change up more against lefties and focus on using his four-seamer more overall.

Target salary (2026) : $789,000 ( Pre-Arbitration )
Team control remaining: 6 years ( Arbitration eligible in 2029, free agent in 2032 )

Executive financial verdict:
Acquiring Rocker provides elite payroll flexibility. Because he remains at the league-minimum salary through 2028, this trade allows the Braves to maintain a high value starting pitcher without impacting our ability to sign other marquee free agents. This completely offsets the loss of the minor league prospects traded away.
